Our story

We started with one question: why does the best part of an iced drink melt first? So we froze our own ice, slowly, until it came out clear and hard enough to last the whole walk home. Everything else followed from there.

Clear ice, always

Frozen for 48 hours in insulated molds, so it melts slower and never waters your drink down.

Ingredients you can name

Single-farm matcha, espresso roasted weekly, fruit we cut every morning. No powders, no syrups from a pump.

Made to linger

Our bars are built for staying: long tables, good light and music turned to conversation level.
